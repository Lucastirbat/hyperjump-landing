# Incorporation guide

How to set up the Delaware C-Corp we invest in, from anywhere in Europe.

<!-- Source: the "Foundations" workshop deck (July 2026). Prices and eligibility are mid-2026 figures. -->

## Why a Delaware C-Corp

We invest through a SAFE in a US Delaware C-Corp. It's the structure US investors expect, so setting it up now also makes your next round simpler. If you already have a company, tell us and we'll confirm whether it works.

**How long it takes:**

- **Forming the company** (steps 1 to 4 below) usually takes 1–2 weeks with a formation service.
- **The EIN** can take longer for founders outside the US: 2–4 weeks, by fax or mail with the IRS. Apply as early as you can.

**Rule of thumb:** start incorporating the day you sign your offer, alongside your visa application. Both can take 4–8 weeks, and they run in parallel with everything else.

## Step by step

1. **Pick a formation service.** Stripe Atlas, Clerky, Firstbase and doola are the common choices for founders doing this remotely. They handle most of the steps below for a flat fee. They're compared [below](#choosing-a-formation-service).
2. **File the Certificate of Incorporation** with the Delaware Division of Corporations. Decide the number of authorized shares and their par value up front. 10,000,000 shares is a common default.
3. **Appoint a registered agent in Delaware.** It's required by law, and usually included by the formation service.
4. **Get an EIN** (Employer Identification Number) from the IRS, with Form SS-4. You need it before you can open a US bank account.
5. **Adopt bylaws and issue founder stock.** Agree the founders' vesting terms at this stage.
6. **File an 83(b) election with the IRS within 30 days of the date your stock is issued.** The deadline is strict and can't be extended. Missing it can create a significant tax bill later.
7. **Open a US business bank account.** Mercury, Brex and Airwallex are common for founders outside the US, and don't need a US address. They're compared [below](#choosing-a-bank).
8. **Set up a cap table tool.** Carta is free to start with. Use it from day one to track the SAFE, the founders' equity and your pit crew's equity.
9. **Sign IP assignment agreements.** Each founder, and any early contractor, assigns their work to the company. That way the company clearly owns all its IP before the investment closes.
10. **Foreign-qualify in your home country** if you'll also operate or hire there. Ask a local accountant whether you need to, for tax and employment.

**Get a warm introduction if you can.** Mercury has tightened approvals for founders outside the US through 2025–26, so a referral matters more now.

### Founders from Ukraine

- **Russia and Belarus are blocked by every provider** (US sanctions). Ukraine isn't: only the occupied regions (Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk, Kherson and Zaporizhzhia) are restricted.
- **Providers judge eligibility by where you live now, not by your passport.** If you have temporary protection in an EU country, you apply as a resident of that country.
- **Mercury, Wise and Airwallex** accept an EIN and a foreign passport, with no SSN needed. Relay needs an SSN or an ITIN.

## Before the SAFE is signed

- **Your company bank account is open.** We wire the investment there.
- **Your cap table is accurate**, with every existing shareholder on it.
- **Your 83(b) election is filed**, within 30 days of the date your stock was issued.
- **You've agreed who signs for the company** on the program agreements.
- **Passport copies are ready** for the identity checks (KYC) on the investment.

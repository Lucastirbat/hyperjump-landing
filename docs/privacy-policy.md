## 2. What we collect, and why

### When you visit our website

If you accept analytics in our cookie banner, we use **PostHog**, an analytics tool, to understand how people use the site. It records:

- which sections you scroll to;
- which links and buttons you click (for example "apply", a scouting tour city, or a FAQ question);
- the page you came from;
- your browser, device type, screen size, and an approximate location (country and city) worked out from your IP address;
- technical errors, so we can fix them;
- a recording of how you move through the page (scrolling, clicks, mouse movement), which we watch to improve the design. The website has no forms, so these recordings contain no text you type.

PostHog sets a cookie and stores an identifier in your browser, so it can tell when you come back. If you decline, none of this happens. You can change your answer at any time with "cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

**Why we're allowed to:** your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Whatever you choose, our hosting provider, Cloudflare, keeps short-lived technical logs of requests (IP address, browser, time), to keep the site running and secure.

**Why we're allowed to:** our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### When you sign in

We use your **email address** to send you a one-time sign-in link. Our sign-in provider keeps technical records of sign-ins (such as the time and the device), to keep your account secure.

**Why we're allowed to:** to give you the account you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### When you apply

For each founder on the application we collect:

- name, email address, phone number, age and country;
- education, work history, awards, and links to your social profiles and GitHub;
- your answers to our questions: something impressive you've built, your plan if accepted, something you did without being told to;
- a photo, if you add one.

About the team and the company we collect the company or project name, team size, how the team met, and your pitch deck (PDF).

When you submit, we automatically tag your application with skills (for example "machine learning" or "robotics") by matching keywords in your answers. These tags help us search applications. They don't decide anything.

**Why we're allowed to:**

- to assess your application before entering into an investment agreement with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR);
- our legitimate interest in finding and selecting founders to invest in (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

You must be 18 or older to apply. We don't knowingly collect applications from anyone younger, and if we find one, we delete it.

### While we review your application

Your application is read by:

- our team;
- members of our pit crew (the mentors listed on our website);
- other reviewers we invite.

Some reviewers only see applications from certain countries or cohorts. Reviewers record scores and notes, and we keep a history of your application's status.

All decisions are made by people. We don't make decisions about you by automated means alone.

### If you're accepted

**On our public website:** we show past jumpers on our website, with each founder's name, short headline, country and photo, and the startup's name, one-line description, website and cohort.

**With our partners:** we share a showcase of our startups with investors and companies we work with, through private links that we can revoke. It includes:

- each founder's name, country, photo, education, work history, awards, profile links and application answers;
- the startup's pitch deck.

Partners never see your email address, phone number or age.

**Startup updates:** to keep track of how our startups are doing, we read:

- messages in the Slack channel we share with your startup;
- emails you send to our team's inbox.

We use **Claude**, an AI model by Anthropic, to summarise them into short internal updates and to write a one-line description of your startup. Our team reviews these summaries. They're internal, except the one-line description, which we may show on our website.

**Why we're allowed to:**

- to carry out our agreement with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR);
- our legitimate interest in supporting and showcasing the startups we invest in (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### The talent pool (only if you opt in)

You can choose to let our partners see your profile as potential talent, for example to be considered for roles at companies we work with. They see your name, country, photo, background and links, never your email, phone number or age.

You can withdraw at any time from your application page, and we stop showing your profile.

**Why we're allowed to:** your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

## 3. Cookies and browser storage

**Our website** stores your answer to the cookie banner (`hj-consent`, kept for a year, then we ask again). That's all it stores, unless you accept analytics. If you accept, PostHog sets a cookie (`ph_…_posthog`, up to a year) and stores the same identifier in your browser's local storage.

**The application platform** stores only what it needs to work:

- your sign-in session, so you stay signed in;
- a note of where you are in the application form, kept until you close the tab.

We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- show you the personal data we hold about you, and give you a copy;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it (including for analytics);
- give it to you in a portable format;
- stop using it where you gave consent, such as website analytics or the talent pool. This doesn't affect what we did before.

Write to [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to your data protection authority. In the EU, that's the authority in the country where you live or work, or where you think the problem happened.

## 8. How we protect it

- Each person on our side sees only what their role needs: applicants, reviewers and admins have different access.
- Pitch decks are stored privately.
- The keys we use to connect to other services are stored encrypted.
- All traffic is encrypted in transit.

Photos are stored so they can be shown on our pages: anyone with a photo's exact link can open it.
